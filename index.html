Common questions

My workouts aren't on my other iPhone. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on for Synergix. Profile → Data shows the sync status.

Health and Coach are locked. Tap Connect Apple Health on the Health tab. Coach needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on.

How do I delete my data? See Deleting your data in the privacy policy.
