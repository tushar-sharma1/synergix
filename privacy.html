Synergix Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short

Synergix has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer can't see anything you log.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Synergix reads your weight, height and Apple Watch workouts on this iPhone to show your BMI, weight trend and workouts. This data stays on this iPhone. It is never uploaded, never synced to iCloud by Synergix and never shared. Synergix writes weight, height, water and protein to Apple Health only when you turn that on.

What you log

Workouts, routines, gyms, custom exercises, water, protein, foods and recipes are saved on this iPhone and sync through your own private iCloud, so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores them in your iCloud account; the developer has no access. Your body details and Coach conversations stay on this iPhone only.

Coach

Coach runs on Apple Intelligence, on this iPhone. Your questions and the data Coach reads are processed on the device and sent nowhere. Coach conversations are kept on this iPhone only.

Reminders and backups

Water and protein reminders are scheduled on this iPhone. Backups are files you create and keep yourself.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes what is on this iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Synergix. Data in Apple Health is managed in the Health app.

Children

Synergix isn't directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the App Store listing before it applies.
